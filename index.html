Wayfable is an AI travel companion. Tell it where you are and how much time you have; it plans a route through the places worth seeing and tells you the story behind each one.

How it works

A plan that fits your time

Enter a destination and a time window. Wayfable orders the stops around opening hours and walking distance, and re-plans if you run late.

Stories you can trust

Every narration is built from cited sources, so the history you hear is history, not guesswork.

Point and ask

Photograph a facade or an inscription to find out what it is, then ask follow-up questions in your own language.

Starting in Kuşadası

Our first destination is Kuşadası, Türkiye's busiest cruise port and the gateway to Ephesus. Many visitors here have only a few hours ashore, which is exactly the kind of day Wayfable is built to plan.
